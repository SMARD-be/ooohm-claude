# Ooo.hm for Claude

Ooo.hm is where electricians draw an electrical installation: the floorplan,
the distribution boards and the single-line diagram, in one connected
workspace. This plugin lets Claude work on your Ooo.hm projects with you.

## What you can ask

- **About a project.** Its supply, boards, circuits and rooms. One circuit at a
  time, or one element: "which breaker is the socket next to the fridge on?"
- **To see it.** Claude shows the floorplan, a board or the single-line
  diagram right in the conversation, with what you asked about marked. Pan,
  zoom or open it full screen.
- **To do the drawing work.** Start a project and draw the floorplan from a
  plan you share: walls, doors, windows, room names and furniture. Put the
  points of an old plan, a photo or a walkthrough on the floorplan, and move,
  turn or resize them where they belong. Group them into circuits. Build the
  distribution boards with their modules, add the breakers, residual-current
  devices and busbars to the single-line diagram, move them there, connect the
  circuits and set what feeds each board. Copy a diagram from paper, or draw one from a
  board that is built. Add a PV installation: inverter, strings, panels,
  optimisers, battery, a model typed in from a datasheet, a string reversed or
  rewired. Link push buttons to a teleruptor and circuits to home automation,
  set cables, rename, re-rate and change the options of what is there.
- **To check before the inspection.** The installation against the Belgian AREI
  rules Ooo.hm has data for, and the PV strings against the inverter's
  datasheet.
- **To wrap up.** A bill of materials, a saved version, the PDF dossier in the
  layout you choose, the share link, the project's documents, a copy of the
  project to continue on, comments for your team and answers to theirs.

Work on the project you have open: say "rename this breaker" and Claude takes
what you selected in Ooo.hm.

## Your drawing stays yours

- You decide what Claude may do when you connect: reading only, or reading and
  editing.
- Every change Claude makes shows in Ooo.hm at once, with an Undo. You can undo
  it for 30 days, under "Assistant changes" in the project menu, or go back to
  a saved version.
- Claude only reaches the projects you can open yourself.

## Good to know

- The installation check prepares you for the inspection. It is not an
  inspection: Ooo.hm is not an accredited inspection body, and every check
  result says so.
- The bill of materials counts elements. It has no cable lengths, brands or
  prices.

## What you need

An Ooo.hm account in an organisation on the Electrician plan. Add the plugin,
connect Ooo.hm when Claude asks, sign in and choose what Claude may do.

## Skills in this plugin

| Skill              | For                                                                                           |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `inspection-prep`  | Checking an installation before the inspection                                                |
| `floorplan-points` | A floorplan from a plan, and its sockets, switches, lights and appliances                     |
| `plan-circuits`    | Circuits, breakers, boards, busbars and the single-line diagram                               |
| `pv-installation`  | Inverter, strings, panels, optimisers, battery, models and string order                       |
| `edit-drawing`     | Renaming, re-rating, rooms, cables, moving, boards and feeds, deleting, unlinking and undoing |
| `materials-list`   | A bill of materials                                                                           |
| `handover-dossier` | The PDF dossier, a saved version, the share link, documents, a copy and comments              |
