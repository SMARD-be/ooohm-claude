# Ooo.hm for Claude

Ooo.hm is where electricians draw an electrical installation: the floorplan,
the distribution boards and the single-line diagram, in one connected
workspace. This plugin lets Claude work on your Ooo.hm projects with you.

## What you can ask

- **About a project.** Its supply, boards, circuits and rooms. One circuit at a
  time, or one element: "which breaker is the socket next to the fridge on?"
- **To see it.** Claude shows the floorplan, a board or the single-line
  diagram right in the conversation, with what you asked about marked. Pan,
  zoom or open it full screen.
- **To do the drawing work.** Put the points of an old plan, a photo or a
  walkthrough on the floorplan. Group them into circuits. Add the breakers and
  residual-current devices to the single-line diagram and connect the circuits.
  Add a PV installation: inverter, strings, panels, battery. Rename and re-rate
  what is there.
- **To check before the inspection.** The installation against the Belgian AREI
  rules Ooo.hm has data for, and the PV strings against the inverter's
  datasheet.
- **To wrap up.** A bill of materials, the PDF dossier, the share link, a
  comment for your team.

Work on the project you have open: say "rename this breaker" and Claude takes
what you selected in Ooo.hm.

## Your drawing stays yours

- You decide what Claude may do when you connect: reading only, or reading and
  editing.
- Every change Claude makes shows in Ooo.hm at once, with an Undo. You can undo
  it for 30 days, under "Assistant changes" in the project menu.
- Claude only reaches the projects you can open yourself.

## Good to know

- The installation check prepares you for the inspection. It is not an
  inspection: Ooo.hm is not an accredited inspection body, and every check
  result says so.
- The bill of materials counts elements. It has no cable lengths, brands or
  prices.
- Walls and rooms are drawn in Ooo.hm itself, and so is the layout of a board.

## What you need

An Ooo.hm account in an organisation on the Electrician plan. Add the plugin,
connect Ooo.hm when Claude asks, sign in and choose what Claude may do.

## Skills in this plugin

| Skill              | For                                                             |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `inspection-prep`  | Checking an installation before the inspection                  |
| `floorplan-points` | Putting sockets, switches, lights and appliances on a floorplan |
| `plan-circuits`    | Circuits, breakers and the single-line diagram                  |
| `pv-installation`  | Inverter, strings, panels and battery                           |
| `edit-drawing`     | Renaming, re-rating, deleting, unlinking and undoing            |
| `materials-list`   | A bill of materials                                             |
| `handover-dossier` | The PDF dossier, a saved version and the share link             |
